PHOENIX — CHANGES AND FIXES
===========================

Newest first. "Windows" is the Windows app, "Mac/Linux" the Electron app for macOS and Linux,
"Android" the phone app. Where a line doesn't name one, it applies to Windows and Mac/Linux.


1.0.2 (not released yet)
------------------------

New
- Background picture: settings → display can put a picture of your own behind the window, showing
  through the theme's background as much as you choose (5–90%). Phoenix keeps its own copy in the
  data folder.
- The player bar's song, cover and progress hide while nothing is playing.
- Now playing: a "generated" tab beside lyrics shows the lyrics the speech model wrote, kept apart
  from a song's own lyrics. It is there while a speech model is downloaded and chosen.
- Now playing: settings → display can put a panel behind the song list and lyrics, so they stay
  readable over the visualizer.
- Lyrics written by a speech model on this computer (whisper.cpp): it listens to a song and writes
  what is sung, with timing, line by line as it hears it. Nothing is sent anywhere. It runs on any
  AMD, Intel or NVIDIA graphics, built in or separate (Vulkan), on Apple chips (Metal), or on the
  processor; you can choose which. Six models from 75 MB to 2.9 GB, with the one that suits your
  graphics card's memory marked as recommended. It can write lyrics as songs play, or for every
  song in the collection.
- Ripping (Windows): FLAC (lossless), and MP3 at 128/192/256/320 kbps, AAC at 128/192/256 kbps and
  WMA at 128/192 kbps, besides WAV.
- Burning (Windows): an MP3 CD (hours of music for MP3 CD players and car stereos; songs that
  aren't MP3s are converted at the quality you choose) or a data disc (the files as they are, on
  a CD, DVD or Blu-ray), besides an audio CD. Files are numbered in burn list order with a
  playlist, and the burn list shows the size and when a DVD or Blu-ray is needed.
- Themes: "follow the system" can use a theme of your own for the system's light side and another
  for its dark side.
- Android: lyrics in now playing. Tap "lyrics" on the picture: from a .lrc or .txt file beside the
  song, its tags, LRCLIB (found by itself if settings → lyrics says so) or the clipboard. Timed
  lyrics follow the song, and tapping a line jumps there.
- Android: settings → playback has an equalizer (the desktop's presets, or ten bands of your own,
  set on the phone's own equalizer) and volume levelling from ReplayGain tags, by song or by album.
- Android: the theme can follow the phone's light or dark setting.

Changed
- The note under the lyrics wraps instead of being cut off, in larger, brighter type.
- Listening for lyrics avoids whisper's habit of repeating a line, splits long sentences into
  lyric-sized lines, uses the song's name as a hint, and weighs several readings on a GPU.
- The installers and packages carry only their own platform's speech-model libraries.

Fixed
- Mac/Linux: asking for generated lyrics a song doesn't have no longer reports an error.


1.0.1 — 2026-11-26
------------------

New
- Keep running in the notification area (Windows) / tray or menu bar (Mac/Linux) when the window
  is closed. Off by default.
- A built-in "system" theme that follows the system's light or dark setting; the title bar follows
  the theme.
- Custom themes, and a designer to make them inside Phoenix.
- Streaming services can go anywhere in the collection, and tabs of your own hold them (set up
  under settings → servers → streaming).
- Mac/Linux caught up with Windows: collection tabs, radio, podcasts and their downloads, sorting
  and filtering, stats, scrobbling, sleep timer, speed, backup, updates, streaming services, tag
  editor, Smart DJ, auto playlists, library care, metadata sources, lyrics, artist photos,
  Mixview, crossfade, equalizer, volume levelling, mini player, media keys, themes, casting (DLNA
  and Chromecast), phone remote, translated names, and moving the data folder.
- Android: settings and pages arranged like the desktop's; radio, podcast sources, sleep timer,
  speed, stats, scrobbling, sorting and filtering, backup and update check. Builds are signed with
  a fixed key.

Fixed
- Mac/Linux: a broken message in settings → storage.
- Windows: the audio client is released properly; internet pictures load on their own lane;
  volume levelling no longer holds the audio lock.
- Radio plays https streams (through the audio engine).


1.0.0 — 2026-11-24
------------------

- First release: the Windows app, the Android app and the Mac/Linux app, with a shared core.
- Smooth scrolling at the display's refresh rate, cover grids and the visualizer drawn with
  Direct2D on Direct3D 11 (Windows).
